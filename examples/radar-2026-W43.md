The largest legacy remitter is buying the rails instead of building them.

## Why it matters
- **Fund portfolio:** Buying from a vendor makes stablecoin settlement a market that incumbents procure. A rails company with a top remitter as a reference customer gets priced differently. Funds holding rival rails companies should expect pressure on their next round.
- **Payments margin:** Mid-size remitters that sell on price lose ground if Western Union cuts its settlement costs. Forkast reports stablecoin payments passed $400B in 8 months and are growing 7x faster than fiat cross-border rails ([Forkast, 18 Oct 2026](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirgFBVV95cUxOVi1jQm1lcEIxVUxhWGVTV0hHMExKU2dtbmw0MGJLbGJ3cENnUXM1WXFpZ3BXZk5fZVFrcHNrSmpsS3FhY0hPR3MwMUNDSTA5SF9YWk5rVDVieDVVTjRUS1V1V0JSUERIU1R2VDM0ZE40U2N2VnV3dGZxQ1FieVBjSFRPUTdUbjdOT1BjUXRDRDN2R2M1dmx1MVcwamJJZTF5QXZIdV9qbEhPZ3daUGc?oc=5)). ## Possible mandates
- **Stablecoin rails companies competing with Noah:** Western Union's choice gives Noah a top reference customer. Rivals may need fresh capital, a sharper niche or a strategic buyer.
- **Mid-size remittance companies with no stablecoin vendor yet:** Western Union has moved first. These companies may need help choosing a vendor, or a partner or buyer that already has the rails.
- **Brazil crypto licence applicants (Crown and the 3 others named by Portal do Bitcoin):** Portal do Bitcoin's piece on what a crypto company costs to run under the new rules (18 Oct) suggests compliance is expensive. Applicants may need capital or senior compliance hires before authorisation.

## Ask an operator
1. When a remitter moves settlement onto stablecoins, where does the saving actually show up: pre-funding and nostro working capital, correspondent fees, or FX spread? And how much of it reaches the customer's price?
2. In Brazil, how long does Central Bank crypto authorisation really take and what does it cost in capital? Is buying an applicant's entity faster than filing your own?
